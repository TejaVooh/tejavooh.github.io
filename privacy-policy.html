Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Tejaanshu Khardekar, an independent consultant based in Pune, Maharashtra, India, handles personal data collected through this website and in the course of consulting engagements. I am the data fiduciary, or data controller, for that information.

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and does no tracking or profiling. There is nothing to consent to, and no banner to dismiss.

2. Service providers

I use a small number of third-party services. Each handles some of your data under its own privacy policy, and each is chosen because it is standard and reputable for its purpose.

  • Hosting. This site is served as static files by a hosting provider that keeps standard access logs.
  • Fonts. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those files directly from Google, which means Google receives your IP address as part of that request.
  • Scheduling. Bookings are handled by Cal.com, which stores the booking details you submit.
  • Video calls. Calls are hosted on Zoom.
  • Calendar. Confirmed bookings are written to my Google Calendar.
  • Email. Correspondence is handled through Google's email service.
  • Payments. Payments, where taken, are processed by a licensed payment provider. I receive confirmation of payment, not your card or bank credentials.

I do not sell personal data, and I do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

3. Transfers outside India

Several of the services above operate internationally, so your data may be processed on servers outside India. I work with clients worldwide, which also means correspondence and engagement material may cross borders in the ordinary course of the work. Where transfers happen, they are to providers that publish their own safeguards for international processing.

4. How long I keep it

  • Enquiries that do not become engagements. Kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.
  • Booking records. Kept for the period the scheduling platform retains them, and in my calendar history.
  • Engagement material. Kept for the duration of the engagement and afterwards only as long as needed, generally up to 3 years, unless the engagement agreement or a non-disclosure agreement requires earlier deletion or longer retention.
  • Financial records. Invoices and payment records are kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law, currently 8 years.

Where a client asks for engagement material to be destroyed at the end of the work, I do so, except for records I am legally required to retain.

5. Confidentiality of engagement material

Information you share during a consulting engagement is treated as confidential under the Terms of Service, and where applicable under a separate non-disclosure agreement. It is used only for the purposes of that engagement and is not disclosed to third parties except where required by law.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal data of yours I hold, and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected, or incomplete data completed;
  • ask for your data to be erased, where I am not required to keep it;
  • withdraw consent you have given, without affecting anything done before you withdrew it;
  • object to or ask me to restrict certain processing;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023;
  • complain to the relevant data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, write to the address in section 8. I respond within 30 days.

7. Security

I take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls on the devices and accounts used for client work, and reliance on established providers for scheduling, email and payments. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

If a breach occurs that is likely to affect you, I will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.
