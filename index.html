Regulated-buyer readiness

Banks, hospitals and insurers approve the demo and then go quiet. Their auditor is the real buyer, and nobody sold to them. I read your product against DPDP, GDPR, ISO 42001 and EU AI Act technical documentation as that auditor will, and resolve data residency questions before the deal stalls.

Security questionnaires & vendor assessments

A two-hundred-question security pack has landed and it is blocking the contract. TPRM, CAIQ, SIG Lite, a bespoke bank questionnaire, or a third party risk assessment of you as an AI vendor. Most people can complete the form. I complete it and close the gaps behind it, because I read the backend first.

Claims & proof

On-device. Guardrails on every call. Continuous evaluation. Replayable audit trail. Each is a claim someone will eventually ask you to evidence. I map every claim, in your model cards and your documentation, to the artifact behind it, the check that re-derives it, and the date it was last true.

AI unit economics & cost-to-serve

Your buyer asks what this costs at scale. So does your investor, with a different motive. I model your real cost per outcome and whether the stack you chose survives the volume you are promising.

What the work turns up.

A privacy policy stated that processing happened on the device. The binary made a call off the device with the user's face attached. Same company, both written within six months, and nobody had placed the two side by side.

A platform sold guardrails on every model call, evaluation before every release, and a replayable audit trail. Three claims, three artifacts, three checks. None of the three could be produced on request.
