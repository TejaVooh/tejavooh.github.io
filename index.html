Technology mandates.

01 / ESTABLISH

Establish Technology Capabilities

Establish technology direction, architecture, engineering functions and operating models for organisations building or expanding their technology capabilities.

Business requirements → Technology strategy → Architecture → Engineering → Operations
  • Technology strategy, target operating models and technology roadmaps.
  • Enterprise architecture, application architecture, data architecture and integration strategy.
  • Cloud infrastructure, platform selection, security architecture and technology standards.
  • Engineering organisation design, technical leadership, development practices and delivery processes.
  • Production operations, CI/CD, observability, service management and operational ownership.
  • Technology capability establishment for GCCs, new ventures and portfolio companies.

Outcome: An accountable technology function, defined architecture, engineering capability and operational ownership.

02 / BUILD & SCALE

Build and Scale Products and Platforms

Develop digital products, enterprise platforms and shared technology capabilities from product definition through production and scale.

Product strategy → Architecture → Engineering → Integration → Production → Scale
  • Product strategy, requirements, capability definition, roadmaps and prioritisation.
  • Application and platform architecture, API design, data models, multi-tenancy and integration patterns.
  • Product engineering, backend and frontend architecture, engineering execution and release management.
  • Enterprise integrations, identity systems, external APIs, data exchange and workflow integration.
  • Performance engineering, availability, observability, infrastructure cost management and scalability.
  • Data platforms, reporting architecture, enterprise search and knowledge systems.
  • AI platform architecture, retrieval-augmented generation, semantic retrieval and agentic workflows.
  • Portfolio technology architecture, reusable components and shared engineering standards across multiple ventures.

Outcome: Production-ready products and platforms with defined architecture, integration, operational and scaling capabilities.

03 / TRANSFORM & ASSESS

Transform and Assess Technology

Assess existing technology environments, identify technical risks, establish target architectures and direct modernisation and remediation.

Current-state assessment → Risk analysis → Target architecture → Remediation → Assurance
  • Technology assessments covering applications, architecture, infrastructure and engineering maturity.
  • Technical due diligence covering codebases, product implementation, dependencies, scalability and technical debt.
  • Application security, access control, data flows, cloud security and technology exposure assessments.
  • AI evaluation covering model performance, retrieval quality, RAG evaluation, system behaviour and evaluation frameworks.
  • AI security, technical governance, evidence requirements and compliance readiness.
  • Technology economics, infrastructure expenditure, cost per customer, compute requirements and capacity planning.
  • Platform modernisation, integration strategy, engineering improvements and transformation roadmaps.

Outcome: Evidence-based technology decisions, identified risks, prioritised remediation and an executable transformation roadmap.
