Capabilities.

Technology direction and delivery, from business objectives and architecture through product development, engineering and operations.

Technology strategy & architecture

Define technology direction, enterprise architecture, platform choices, integration patterns and technical standards. Establish a practical roadmap that connects business priorities with the capabilities, systems and investment required to deliver them.

Product & platform leadership

Translate business requirements into product strategy, roadmaps and delivery priorities. Lead 0→1 development, platform evolution and modernisation across product, engineering, data, APIs and user-facing applications.

Engineering & technology operations

Establish engineering practices, delivery governance, operational resilience, performance measurement and technology standards. Align teams, vendors and infrastructure around reliable delivery and sustainable operating models.

AI platforms, governance & assurance

Assess and develop AI-enabled products and platforms, including RAG, semantic retrieval and agentic workflows. Establish evaluation, security, governance and compliance practices that make system behaviour measurable and suitable for enterprise adoption.

Technology assessment & transformation

Review architecture, implementation, dependencies, security, scalability and operating costs. Identify technical risks and priorities for investment, modernisation, integration or organisational change, with findings tied to evidence and actionable decisions.

Business applications.

Establishing technology capabilities Define the technology operating model, architecture, delivery practices and initial team capabilities required to support a new business, venture or organisational unit.
Building and scaling digital products Move from business concept to product roadmap, architecture and production delivery. Improve platform capability, integration, reliability and the economics of operating the product.
Modernising enterprise platforms Assess existing systems and dependencies, define the target architecture and prioritise changes to improve maintainability, interoperability, performance, security and operational resilience.
Technology investment and due diligence Examine what a technology business has built, how it operates, what it depends on and what it will take to scale. Provide an evidence-based view of technical strengths, liabilities and investment requirements.
AI adoption and operationalisation Identify appropriate AI use cases, design platform and data requirements, establish evaluation and governance, and integrate AI capabilities into products and business workflows.
