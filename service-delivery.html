4. Where I work

Services are delivered remotely and are available to clients worldwide. On-site work is possible by separate arrangement, with travel and accommodation agreed in advance and billed at cost.

Calls are scheduled in your local timezone. My working base is India Standard Time, and I hold hours that overlap with European and North American business hours.

If a call cannot proceed, a link does not work, or a deliverable does not reach you, write to tejaanshukhardekar@gmail.com. I respond within 2 working days and will either redeliver or reschedule.
