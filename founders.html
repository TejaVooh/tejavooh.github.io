For founders and CXOs.

Sooner or later someone opens your AI product. A buyer's security team. A regulator or an auditor. An investor conducting technical diligence. None of them attended your pitch, none of them are reading your marketing, and each is looking for something different.

What they have in common is that they assess the product against what you have stated about it. Documentation, privacy pages, model cards, evaluation results, security questionnaires and architecture diagrams are written by different people at different times, and are almost never reconciled against each other, or against the running system.

That gap is where deals stall, audits extend, and diligence becomes a second round of questions that nobody budgeted for.

I open it first, and report what they will find. Ordinarily it is not what the team expected.
