For founders and CXOs.

Sooner or later someone opens your AI product. A buyer's security team. A regulator or an auditor. An investor running technical diligence. None of them were at your pitch, none of them are reading your marketing, and each is looking for something different.

What they have in common is that they check the product against what you said about it. Documentation, privacy pages, model cards, evaluation results, security questionnaires and architecture diagrams get written by different people at different times, and almost never checked against each other, or against the running system.

That gap is where deals stall, audits drag, and diligence turns into a second round of questions nobody budgeted for.

I open it first, and tell you what they will find. Usually it is not what the team expected.

What follows is the pattern, never the company.
