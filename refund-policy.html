Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how cancellations, rescheduling and refunds are handled for consultations booked through this website and for consulting engagements with Tejaanshu Khardekar, Pune, Maharashtra, India. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

1. Free discovery calls

Discovery calls are offered free of charge. No payment is taken, so no refund arises. You may cancel or reschedule at any time using the link in your booking confirmation email.

2. Paid consultations

Paid consultations are charged in advance at the rate shown at the time of booking. The following applies:

SituationOutcome
You cancel more than 24 hours before the scheduled startFull refund, or reschedule at no cost
You cancel within 24 hours of the scheduled startNo refund. One complimentary reschedule offered within 30 days
You do not attend and do not notify meNo refund
I cancel or am unable to attendFull refund, or reschedule at your choice
The call cannot proceed due to a technical failure on my sideFull refund, or reschedule at your choice

Rescheduling more than 24 hours before the scheduled start is free and unlimited. Please use the link in your confirmation email so the change reaches my calendar.

3. Consulting engagements

Engagements such as readiness reviews, viability reviews and embedded product leadership are governed by the written proposal or statement of work for that engagement. Where that document is silent, the following applies:

  • Before work begins. If you cancel before I have started work, any advance paid is refunded in full, less any third-party costs already committed on your behalf.
  • After work begins. If you cancel partway through, you are charged for work performed up to the date of written notice, calculated on the agreed basis for that engagement. Any balance of an advance beyond that amount is refunded.
  • Fixed-scope engagements. Once the agreed deliverable has been issued, the fee for that deliverable is non-refundable.
  • Retained and ongoing engagements. Either party may terminate with the notice period stated in the proposal. Fees for notice periods already served are not refundable.
  • If I terminate for reasons other than your breach of the Terms, you are refunded any amount paid for work not yet performed.
Advisory work is judgement, not a guaranteed outcome. Dissatisfaction with a conclusion, recommendation or finding is not by itself grounds for a refund, particularly where the finding is that a product, market or approach is not viable. That answer is often the most valuable result of the work.

4. How refunds are processed

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Refunds cannot be redirected to a different account or instrument.
  • Refunds are initiated within 5 working days of approval.
  • Once initiated, the time for funds to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, and is typically 5 to 10 working days for domestic payments and longer for international ones.
  • Refunds are made in the original currency of payment. For international payments, the amount received may differ from the amount originally paid because of exchange-rate movement and bank charges. These differences are outside my control and are not reimbursed.
  • Payment gateway fees, currency conversion charges and bank transfer costs are not refundable.
  • Applicable taxes are refunded along with the refunded amount where the law permits.

5. How to request a cancellation or refund

Send a request to tejaanshukhardekar@gmail.com with your name, the date and time of the booking or the engagement reference, the payment reference, and the reason for the request.

Requests are acknowledged within 2 working days and a decision is communicated within 5 working days of receipt.

6. Disputes

If you are unhappy with how a refund request has been handled, write to me first at the address above so it can be resolved directly. Any dispute not resolved that way is governed by the law and jurisdiction set out in the Terms of Service.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The version in force at the date of your booking or engagement applies to it. The current version is always published on this page with its last-updated date.
